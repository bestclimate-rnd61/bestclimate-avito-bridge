   - `Тепловая завеса Ballu 5 кВт | Магазин / офис`
3. `CURTAIN-6-MIX`
   - `Тепловая завеса 6 кВт | Подбор + монтаж`
4. `CURTAIN-9-MIX`
   - `Тепловая завеса 9 кВт | Для магазина`
5. `CURTAIN-12-TEPLOMASH`
   - `Тепловая завеса 12 кВт | Коммерческие помещения`
6. `CURTAIN-18-TEPLOMASH`
   - `Тепловая завеса 18 кВт | Склад / магазин`

### Why not more at launch
Power, doorway size, mounting height, 220/380 V and required air throw matter more than simply having dozens of brand duplicates. Start with six intent clusters, then split winners by brand/model.

### First-screen qualifier questions
Every curtain ad should push the buyer to send:
- doorway width;
- doorway height;
- supply voltage 220/380 V;
- shop / office / warehouse / vestibule;
- whether installation is needed.

This improves lead quality and lets the seller recommend the correct model rather than competing only on the cheapest sticker price.
