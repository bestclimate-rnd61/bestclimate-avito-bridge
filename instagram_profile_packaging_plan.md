## Premium cover system
Safe for 9:16 Story and circular Highlight crop.
- 1080x1920 master canvas.
- Primary icon/title centered inside a conservative circular safe area; avoid important text near outer edges.
- One visual language across all Highlights: minimal, premium, high contrast, no clutter.
- Short labels only: СТАРТ / КЕЙСЫ / AI / ИНСТРУМЕНТЫ / РАЗБОР / ОБО МНЕ / FAQ.
- Prepare artwork before publishing; public Stories/Highlights require explicit approval.

## Feed visual system
Use 3 repeatable cover families:
1. FACE + CLAIM — portrait/object + 3–5 word promise.
2. SYSTEM — simple diagram/process + 3–5 word title.
3. CASE — before/after or metric + evidence cue.

Rules: high contrast; one focal point; consistent typography; no decorative noise; mobile-readable; no watermarks from other platforms.

## Reels production rules
- Original material or meaningfully transformed material.
- 1080x1920 vertical master.
- First-frame hook in first 1–2 seconds.
- Captions and readable on-screen text.
- Fast pacing without removing comprehension.
- Cover: 3–5 readable words.
- CTA optimized for save/send/follow when appropriate.
- Track watch time/retention, sends per reach, saves, profile visits and follows after launch.
- Trial Reels: use only if available in the Instagram app/account; treat as a manual/native experiment, not a Buffer automation dependency.
