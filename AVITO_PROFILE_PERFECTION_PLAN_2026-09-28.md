# Best Climate Rostov — Avito Profile Perfection Plan — 2026-09-28

## Objective
Turn the Avito profile into the strongest HVAC storefront possible: maximum trust, maximum conversion, clean catalog architecture, consistent visuals, fast response, correct delivery/payment/warranty information, and measurable scaling by product/brand/city.

This plan is not limited to ads. It covers the entire profile: identity, avatar/logo, banner/header, company description, catalog/navigation, delivery, guarantees, payment, reviews, messaging, photos, analytics, Autoload, and scaling.

## Operating rule
Work never stops at one finished task. After each completed block:
1. verify result;
2. inspect the next highest-impact bottleneck;
3. execute the next safe improvement;
4. measure impact;
5. retain winners and replace weak elements.

No paid promotion or destructive live changes are enabled automatically without a clear reason and verification.

## Tariff-dependent storefront conclusion — verified 2026-09-28
Current public 2026 references describing Avito business tariff capabilities consistently indicate:
- Expanded/advanced business packages can provide a professional profile and, in some configurations/categories, remove recommendations of other sellers from the user's ad pages;
- Maximum packages add the strongest brand storefront features: logo in search/listings, branded profile banner/carousel, showcase, unique profile URL and competitor/recommendation suppression where supported;
- exact availability and price are account/category specific and must be verified in the live Avito tariff screen before any paid upgrade.

Execution implication:
1. audit the user's CURRENT paid package before spending anything;
2. if Maximum features are already included, enable every included non-paid profile feature;
3. if competitor hiding/banner/showcase/logo require a higher package, report the exact missing feature and price first — do not purchase automatically;
4. do not pay twice for capabilities already included in the current subscription.

## A. Profile identity and first screen

### 1. Profile name
Primary positioning should immediately explain the category and geography.
Working direction:
**Бест Климат Ростов | Кондиционеры • Вентиляция • Тепловые насосы**

Keep within Avito limits and avoid stuffing if the interface truncates. If only a shorter name is allowed, use:
**Бест Климат Ростов**

### 2. Avatar / logo
Target:
- readable at tiny mobile size;
- no small text;
- one strong snowflake/sun or BC/BCR mark;
- dark premium background;
- high contrast;
- same identity on Avito, site, WhatsApp/Telegram and social pages.

Avoid circular logos with many tiny words because they become unreadable in Avito search/profile cards.

### 3. Banner / profile header
If current Avito tariff exposes a profile banner/header, create a premium wide banner with one core promise only:
**КОНДИЦИОНЕРЫ • ТЕПЛОВЫЕ НАСОСЫ • ВЕНТИЛЯЦИЯ**
Secondary line:
**Продажа • Монтаж • Сервис • Доставка по РФ**
Trust strip:
**Монтаж 2 года | Оборудование 3–7 лет | Свой склад / производство** where factually applicable.

Do not overload the banner with phone numbers if Avito rules/interface make them undesirable. Use profile CTA and messaging instead.

### 4. Company description
Recommended structure:
- who we are;
- what we sell/install;
- geography;
- why trust us;
- delivery/payment;
- warranty;
- CTA.

Working copy:
**Бест Климат Ростов — продажа, подбор, монтаж и обслуживание климатического оборудования для квартир, домов, офисов и коммерческих объектов.**

**Основные направления:** настенные, кассетные, канальные и напольно‑потолочные кондиционеры; тепловые насосы воздух‑воздух; VRF/VRV; вентиляция; бризеры и рекуператоры; корзины, кронштейны и подставки; ремонт и обслуживание.

Работаем по Ростову‑на‑Дону и области, отправляем оборудование по РФ. Подбираем решение по площади, бюджету и задачам объекта. Есть наличный и безналичный расчёт, QR/перевод, кредит/рассрочка — только если доступно на момент сделки.

Монтаж выполняется с вакуумированием, качественными материалами и гарантией. Для объектов можем подготовить несколько вариантов оборудования и смету.

**Напишите площадь помещения, город и задачу — предложим подходящие варианты и рассчитаем стоимость.**

Delivery must be described consistently across relevant ads and profile settings.

### Local

- free delivery only when the actual offer qualifies;

### Russia-wide
- transport-company dispatch for goods that can ship;
- buyer/seller payment responsibility stated clearly per offer;
- packaging / two-box equipment specifics handled in order communication;
- no claim of free Russia-wide shipping unless the specific campaign supports it.

### Product types with strong shipping potential
- heat pumps;
- household split systems;
- air curtains;
- baskets/brackets/stands;
- semi-industrial equipment where logistics are practical.
