# Heat Pump Creative Rules — 2026-09-28

## Campaign priority
1. AQUA TOWADA — priority 1, maximum scaling.
2. Midea heat-pump SKUs — priority 2.
3. Gree heat-pump SKUs — priority 2.
4. Other verified heat-pump SKUs (including Ballu and other lines) — prepare after model-level validation.

## Mandatory first-image rule
For every selected SKU that is genuinely intended/approved for heating down to -30 °C, the first image must show a large, highly visible claim:

**ОБОГРЕВ ДО -30 °C**

This claim should be one of the first visual anchors and must not be hidden in secondary text.

Recommended visual hierarchy:
1. Brand + model/series
2. ТЕПЛОВОЙ НАСОС ВОЗДУХ-ВОЗДУХ
3. ОБОГРЕВ ДО -30 °C
4. Area / capacity
5. БЕСПЛАТНАЯ ДОСТАВКА

## Delivery USP
Use consistently where applicable:
**БЕСПЛАТНАЯ ДОСТАВКА ПО РОССИИ, ЛНР, ДНР, КРЫМУ И АБХАЗИИ**

## Click-cost discipline
- Do not optimize for maximum raw clicks.
- Optimize for target clicks, contacts and sales.
- Show truthful current selling price or a truthful documented discount.
- Do not use fabricated crossed-out prices.
- Separate city / intent / model where doing so improves relevance.
- Main CTA: ask for city + room/house area for selection and calculation.

## Publishing rule
Do not publish a model as '-30 °C' unless that exact SKU is in the selected heat-pump list approved for that heating range. Do not blindly apply the claim to unrelated models of the same brand.
