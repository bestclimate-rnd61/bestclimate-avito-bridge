# Best Climate Rostov — Avito HVAC Scaling Plan — 2026-09-28

## Goal
Scale only after the current managed-migration batch is safe. Build controlled Avito portfolios for:
1. air-to-air heat pumps;
2. cassette AC;
3. floor-ceiling AC;
4. air curtains.

All new listings follow the same rules already locked for the migration project:
- no invented specs;
- no unsupported low-temperature claims;
- no teaser price unless a real SKU exists at that price;
- no automatic paid promotion;
- unique city/title/copy combinations rather than unmanaged duplicate spam;
- concrete SKU/brand rows are preferred where Avito requires a single Vendor.

---

## 1. Air-to-air heat pumps

### Core launch brands
**AQUA, Ballu, GREE, Midea.**

A fifth brand is optional and should be added only after current model/price evidence and Avito demand justify it. Do not force a fifth brand merely to reach five listings.

### Verified product anchors
#### AQUA TOWADA
Current market listings confirm active TOWADA Premium Inverter models including:
- AQI-35FIS1/R3-W;
- AQI-50FIS1/R3-W.

Best Climate already has internal/product knowledge for TOWADA 25-class as well. Exact claims (working temperature, warranty, Wi-Fi, noise, factory, etc.) must be copied only from the verified model specification used in each ad.

#### Ballu ICE PEAK
Ballu's own catalogue positions ICE PEAK as an air-to-air heat pump with operation on heating down to -30 °C. This is a strong low-temperature campaign anchor, but the exact model must be selected before publication.

#### GREE
Brand stays in the launch pool, but the exact current -25/-30 °C model and SKU must be verified before feed generation. Do not use generic “GREE -30” wording without the model spec.

#### Midea
Brand stays in the launch pool, but exact low-temperature model/SKU must be verified before feed generation. Do not infer operating limits from another series.

### Recommended Avito portfolio structure
Start with **one strong listing per brand**, not five brands mixed into one product listing. Avito household AC schema requires a single `Vendor`, so individual brand listings are cleaner for search and Autoload.

Initial campaign IDs:
- `HP-AQUA-TOWADA`
- `HP-BALLU-ICEPEAK`
- `HP-GREE-LOWTEMP`
- `HP-MIDEA-LOWTEMP`

Inside each listing:
- exact model family;
- actual available sizes/power classes;
- heating design temperature only if verified for that model;
- equipment price separately from installation where applicable;
- areas are recommendations, not universal promises;
- delivery / installation geography stated truthfully.

### First-title frameworks
Titles must stay model-led and search-led, e.g.:
- `Тепловой насос AQUA TOWADA | Обогрев и охлаждение`
- `Тепловой насос Ballu ICE PEAK | До -30 °C`
- `Тепловой насос GREE | Инвертор | Монтаж`
- `Тепловой насос Midea | Инвертор | Монтаж`

Temperature goes in title only when verified for the exact advertised series.

### Testing matrix
For every brand test:
- CTR / views;
- favorites;
- contacts;
- contact conversion;
- contact cost;
- qualified leads;
- sold equipment / installations.

After 2–4 weeks, keep winners and split only if data supports separate power/model ads.

## 2. Cassette AC

### Brand shortlist for research and launch
- GREE
- Haier
- MDV
- Ballu
- Midea

Do not publish all five automatically. Final launch set should be 3–5 brands based on current stock, margin, supplier reliability and Avito demand.

### Priority capacity bands
The most useful semi-industrial search bands for the campaign are:
- 18k BTU (~5.0–5.3 kW);
- 24k BTU (~6.8–7.1 kW);
- 36k BTU (~9.3–10.6 kW);
- 48k BTU (~13.5–14.1 kW);
- 60k BTU (~15.5–17.6 kW).

These bands are widely represented in manufacturer lines and map well to commercial premises.

### Campaign architecture
Prefer **power-led + application-led** listings rather than copying five brand catalogs:
- `CASSETTE-24`
- `CASSETTE-36`
- `CASSETTE-48`
- `CASSETTE-60`

Each ad can mention available brands in the description, while the actual product row/feed entry must use the concrete Vendor/SKU being sold.

Suggested title frameworks:
- `Кассетный кондиционер 24 | Монтаж под ключ`
- `Кассетный кондиционер 36 | Для магазина и офиса`
- `Кассетный кондиционер 48 | Подбор + монтаж`
- `Кассетный кондиционер 60 | Коммерческие объекты`
