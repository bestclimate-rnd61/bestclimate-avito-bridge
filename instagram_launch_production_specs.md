## PIN 1 — ai-reel-001-start — POSITIONING
Goal: convert profile visitors into followers by proving this is a build-in-public implementation account, not generic AI commentary.
Target duration: 22–28 sec.

### Shot list
0.0–1.5s — Tight talking-head, direct eye contact. On-screen: `Я НЕ РАССКАЗЫВАЮ ПРО AI СО СТОРОНЫ`.
1.5–4.0s — Laptop/phone B-roll with real workflow screens, no secrets visible. Voice: `Я внедряю его в свои бизнес-процессы.`
4.0–8.0s — Fast 3-shot montage: incoming lead / content workflow / analytics dashboard. Labels: `ЗАЯВКИ`, `КОНТЕНТ`, `АНАЛИТИКА`.
8.0–14.0s — Talking head. `Здесь буду показывать, что именно автоматизировал, сколько это стоило по времени и деньгам и где AI ошибается.`
14.0–20.0s — Screen/B-roll of an automation flow. On-screen: `БЕЗ МАГИИ. С ЦИФРАМИ.`
20.0–26.0s — Talking head CTA: `Если у тебя есть бизнес — подпишись. Соберём рабочую AI-систему по шагам.`

### Cover
Main: `AI В РЕАЛЬНОМ БИЗНЕСЕ`
Small: `строю систему публично`
Visual: Alexander portrait on one side; clean workflow/UI fragments behind, no fake metrics.

### Edit notes
Hard cuts every 1–2.5 sec; captions throughout; music low under voice; no stock robots/neon brains; preserve natural skin/voice; show only verifiable interfaces and blur secrets/client PII.

## PIN 2 — ai-reel-003-audit — LEAD MAGNET
Goal: create demand for the free AI business audit, but do not promise a live form until the funnel exists.
Target duration: 25–32 sec.

### Shot list
0.0–1.5s — Talking head. On-screen: `7 ОТВЕТОВ → ЧТО ОТДАТЬ AI ПЕРВЫМ?`
1.5–5.0s — Simple visual list flashes: leads / content / analytics / repeat sales.
5.0–11.0s — Voice: `Автоматизировать всё подряд — ошибка. Сначала ищем 2–3 процесса, где теряются время или деньги.`
11.0–18.0s — Show audit logic card: `процесс → потери → метрика → автоматизация → контроль`.
18.0–25.0s — Talking head: `Я собираю бесплатный стартовый AI-разбор бизнеса из семи вопросов.`
25.0–31.0s — Safe pre-launch CTA: `Сохрани ролик. Когда форма будет готова — открою первые разборы здесь.`

### Cover
Main: `ЧТО ОТДАТЬ AI ПЕРВЫМ?`
Badge: `7 вопросов`
Visual: Alexander + minimal audit checklist.

### Funnel gate
Do NOT use `Напиши РАЗБОР` as the public CTA until Telegram/SaleBot intake can actually receive, qualify and store the lead. Before that, use save/follow CTA only.

## PIN 3 — ai-reel-011-case — PROOF
Goal: establish evidence standard. Publish only after a real case has verifiable before/after data.
Target duration: 30–40 sec.

### Required evidence before production
- named or anonymized business/process with permission;
- baseline metric and measurement window;
- exact automation scope;
- implementation/tool cost where shareable;
- after metric with comparable window;
- at least one limitation/failure/lesson.

### Storyboard template
0.0–2.0s — `КЕЙС БЕЗ КРАСИВЫХ ПРОЦЕНТОВ`.
2.0–7.0s — Problem + baseline metric.
7.0–14.0s — What was automated, with real workflow proof.
14.0–22.0s — Cost/time to implement.
22.0–30.0s — Result with before/after visual.
30.0–36.0s — What failed / what remained manual.
36.0–40.0s — CTA to follow next experiment.

### Cover
Main: `КЕЙС: ДО → ПОСЛЕ`
Small: `цифры + ошибки`
Never place an unverified percentage on the cover.

## Shared production checklist
- 9:16 vertical; key text inside safe central area.
- First meaningful spoken/visual hook <=1.5 sec.
- Burned-in Russian captions, readable without sound.
- One idea per Reel; one primary CTA.
- No fake dashboards, fabricated revenue, fabricated clients, or unverifiable performance claims.
- Remove/blur passwords, API keys, customer contacts and personal data.
- Draft media stays outside Buffer until user approval; queue item remains `approved=false` and `media_url=""`.
