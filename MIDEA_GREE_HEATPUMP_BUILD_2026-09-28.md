## PPC rules for all heat-pump ads
- Do not chase maximum clicks; optimize for qualified contact rate and CPL.
- One search intent per listing.
- Use exact model/series in title where possible.
- Put -30°C and free delivery on first image to pre-qualify clicks.
- Show real selling price or a truthful starting price only.
- Do not create fake crossed-out old prices or fake discount percentages.
- CTA: `Напишите площадь помещения + город`.
- Track: clicks → contacts → CPL → sale → gross margin → net profit.
