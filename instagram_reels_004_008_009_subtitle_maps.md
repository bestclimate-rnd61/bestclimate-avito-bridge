## Global production rules
- Canvas/export: 1080x1920 (9:16).
- Keep critical text inside mobile-safe center area; do not place key words against top/bottom UI zones.
- First-frame promise must be readable within ~1 second.
- Subtitles: short semantic chunks, normally 1–2 lines; emphasize one idea at a time.
- On-screen headline and spoken subtitle must not compete: headline = claim/structure, subtitles = speech.
- Avoid unverified numeric promises, fake case metrics, income guarantees, or claims that the audit funnel is already live.
- End CTA remains save/send/follow until the AI-audit intake passes E2E testing.
