# Market research — HVAC / Avito optimization — 2026-09-28

## Purpose
Use current Russian market benchmarks to calibrate pricing, offers and first-screen positioning for Best Climate Rostov listings. This file is research only; it does not publish or modify Avito ads.

## Key market benchmarks

### Split systems / household AC
- Krasnodar retail market currently shows a broad budget floor around 14,700–16,000 RUB for entry-level units, with common brands around 18,500–24,000 RUB and inverter products commonly starting around 19,900–23,400 RUB.
- Moscow promotional retail examples show entry-level on/off split systems around 18,900 RUB before installation. Full installed offers are typically much higher once equipment + standard install are included.
- Current Moscow market commentary places decent inverter 7k systems roughly around 28,000–45,000 RUB for equipment alone, with total turnkey cost materially higher.

### Installation
- Moscow standard installation is commonly packaged separately from equipment; current market references show professional standard install as a meaningful additional line item.
- Krasnodar competitors advertise installation from roughly 5,500 RUB, making installation price a strong click driver there.

### Heat pumps air-to-air down to -30C
- Current specialist retail market shows -30C models in roughly the high-50k to mid-70k range for 20–40 m² class equipment, with installation often quoted separately around 17,000 RUB.
- Competitive products in this range include Electrolux Avalanche, Ballu Ice Peak and Royal Clima Felicita.
- Best Climate's 65,400–79,990 RUB pricing is market-plausible; conversion should be improved primarily via clearer model/area/install positioning rather than aggressive price cuts.

### AC baskets / facade screens
- Factory/wholesale competitors currently advertise roughly 2,500 RUB wholesale at the low end, around 3,822 RUB from another manufacturer, and around 4,400 RUB from another industrial supplier.
- Best Climate's 2,600 RUB wholesale price is therefore highly competitive; 3,500 RUB Moscow price is also within market range.
- Winning positioning should emphasize own production, batch pricing, custom dimensions, RAL, lead time and Russia-wide shipping.

## Listing strategy derived from market + account stats

### Priority 1 — scale proven demand
1. AC baskets / facade screens
   - Keep 2,600 RUB wholesale anchor in Rostov if genuinely available.
   - Keep 3,500 RUB Moscow offer if it reflects a real base model.
   - Expand unique city versions; avoid identical duplicates.
   - First image: product + OWN PRODUCTION + WHOLESALE + RAL/CUSTOM SIZE.

2. Ventilation / VRF
   - Current Avito contact conversion is exceptionally strong despite low views.
   - Main objective is traffic growth, not offer rewrite.
   - Expand city/region coverage with unique text and object-specific use cases.

3. Taganrog AC with installation
   - Strong contact conversion. Preserve core offer; test first photo/title before large text changes.

### Priority 2 — improve high-interest / low-contact ads
1. Heat pumps
   - Market pricing is acceptable.
   - Add explicit area/model/temperature/install explanation in first screen.
   - Separate equipment price from installation where needed.
   - Use -30C, inverter, Wi-Fi, warranty, heating + cooling as primary visual hierarchy when factually applicable.

2. Brackets / stands
   - Views are adequate but contacts are weak.
   - Reposition toward wholesale buyers/installers and show unit + batch price logic clearly.

### Priority 3 — rebuild low-visibility city AC ads
Moscow, Voronezh, Krasnodar city-generic AC ads currently have almost no views. The issue is not just price; generic titles and weak differentiation are likely suppressing click-through. Rebuild around real stock, installation, warranty and exact city intent.

Recommended title framework:
- Moscow: `Кондиционеры в Москве от 16 700 ₽ | Установка`
- Voronezh: `Кондиционеры в Воронеже от 16 700 ₽ | Монтаж`
- Krasnodar: `Кондиционеры в Краснодаре от 16 700 ₽ | Монтаж`

Only retain the 16,700 RUB anchor where an actual item at that price is available.

## Guardrails
- Do not invent factory, warranty, operating-temperature, noise, SEER/SCOP or area claims.
- Do not use a low teaser price unless the corresponding product/service is genuinely available at that price.
- Preserve high-performing listings until an A/B replacement is ready.
- Avoid creating unmanaged duplicate Avito listings while existing ads have no Autoload ad_id mapping.
